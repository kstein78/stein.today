Relograph

A monthly emigration index for Russian speakers: a ranking of 20 countries, fact-checked articles and video, real prices reported by the community. Prototype v2, looking for an investor.

Relograph helps Russian speakers who are thinking of moving abroad compare countries on data rather than rumours.

Every month it ranks 20 countries across seven categories: cost of living, visas and residence permits, banking for Russians and Belarusians, integration and education, quality of life, community, and citizenship. The methodology and raw data are published.

Next to the ranking are articles, field reports and video, each piece fact-checked. Prices come from the community: photos of price tags sent through a Telegram bot and checked by a moderator before they enter the index.

The second prototype is live. I am looking for an investor and for partners: experts and correspondents in different countries.
