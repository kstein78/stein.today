divno.rs

A marketplace for antiques, vintage and swaps across Serbia and the former Yugoslavia. No commissions, no paid boosts.

divno is where people buy, sell and swap old things: furniture, ceramics, toys, watches, books, coins and records.

You can ask flea-market hunters to find a specific item, or ask experts what an object is and what it is worth. Helpful work earns “dukati”, a reputation score that shows who can be trusted.

Listings can be posted on the site, through a Telegram bot, or by linking to KupujemProdajem, Kupindo or eBay. Five languages: Serbian (Latin and Cyrillic), Russian, English and German.

The eight-pointed star in the logo comes from the patterns of Pirot carpets, which are on Serbia’s intangible cultural heritage list.
