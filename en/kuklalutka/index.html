Kukla·Lutka

Our family collection, mine and my wife’s: more than 1,500 antique, vintage and rare dolls. We show, buy and trade.

The site shows part of the collection my wife and I build together. Dolls from Yugoslavia, Germany, Italy, Romania, Poland, the USSR and a few from the USA.

We find them at flea markets, through other collectors and in old attics. Sometimes we rehome dolls with new collectors, and we buy and trade dolls in any condition, including ones that need restoration.

Prices are shown in RSD, EUR, USD and RUB. Languages: Russian, Serbian, English and German.
